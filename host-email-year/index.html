Host email year

What to send hosts and when, in order. Every email is prepared a week before it goes out: tested with the hubs, then scheduled. So each date below has two parts, the day it must be ready and the day it sends.

Tags record factsA tag says what is true about a person (registered in 2027, event in week 3). It is added once.
Segments are what you send toEvery email goes to a saved segment, never to a tag. The segment updates itself.
Opt out through preferencesHosts who want fewer emails use Update preferences, not Unsubscribe, so they stay on the list.
Hub test contacts. Each prepared email goes first, as a test, to one contact from each hub. They give feedback during the week, and they see what is going out on the regional list so they can change or hold what they send to their own local lists. Likely contacts are Louise, Laura and Anna, but check with each team who it should go to before the first test.
Tags: being fixed, check before the first send. The website tags new hosts Host 2027, but the saved segments below look for host-2027. Until Dan confirms the segments match, a registered host could still get a reminder. The website also adds no week tag yet, so the week segments stay empty until that is sorted.

The May email: the opt-out

The report and celebration email is where hosts are told why they are on the list and how to step back. Keep these points in it:

You are on this list because you hosted this year or recently, or showed interest. If you would rather not be reminded next year, tell us. If you do not register next year you will only get the registrations-open style emails, not the weekly host emails. To change what you receive, use Update preferences at the bottom of this email, not Unsubscribe.
