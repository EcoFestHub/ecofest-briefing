Between the emails

Every Monday

The host list sheet emails you a summary. Open it and look at three things:

  • Hub opt-ins to add: paste the people into the KP Mailchimp, then use Host list > Mark hub opt-ins as added.
  • Updates for Mailchimp: paste columns A to D into the regional import, choose Update existing contacts, then put Y in Done.
  • To check rows on Contacts: decide a status for each.

When a host is missing from a segment

Open the person in Mailchimp and look at their tags. A registered host needs host-2027 and a week-2027-N tag. If the tag is missing, add it by hand and tell Dan, because the registration tagging may have failed.

The May email: the opt-out

The report and celebration email is where hosts are told why they are on the list and how to step back. Keep these points in it:

You are on this list because you hosted this year or recently, or showed interest. If you would rather not be reminded next year, tell us. If you do not register next year you will only get the registrations-open style emails, not the weekly host emails. To change what you receive, use Update preferences at the bottom of this email, not Unsubscribe.
