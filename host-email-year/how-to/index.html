How to use the host list

The host list sheet does two main jobs: it gives you the right list of people to email, and it passes your corrections on to Mailchimp. Pick the job below and follow the steps.

The two main jobs

Get a list of people to emailFor example: I need to email the EcoFest North hosts.
  1. Open the EcoFest host contact master list in Google Drive.
  2. Update it from Mailchimp. Open the Host list menu and choose Refresh regional export (API). Wait for the message at the bottom right.
    • No access to the API? Paste a Mailchimp export into the Raw export tab (with its header row), then choose Read pasted Mailchimp export.
  3. Choose Run checks and update. In report-only mode it changes nothing; it only tells you what it found.
  4. Choose Build send audiences.
  5. Open the Audiences tab. The table on the right (columns F to H) has one row per list, with a count and the emails. Click a list name and a small note explains what it is.
  6. Pick your list (see the table below), copy the emails cell, and paste it into the BCC box of a new Outlook email.

People on the Suppression tab never appear in any list. Only people with a status of Active (or Active hub opt-in) appear. Registered hosts are on the regional list, so they get the regional emails and you will rarely email them from here.
Pass my changes on to MailchimpWhen I correct a name, phone number or email here and the person is also in the regional Mailchimp.
  1. Make the change on the Contacts tab, one cell at a time (email, first name, last name or phone).
  2. Open the separate Updates for Mailchimp tab. It lists only the people you changed who are on the regional list. It is not the Contacts tab.
  3. On that tab, select columns A to D of the rows without a Y, and copy.
  4. In the regional Mailchimp (the Mailchimp website, not a sheet tab) go to Audience, All contacts, Add contacts, Import contacts, then the copy and paste option. Paste, and match the columns to Email, First name, Last name and Phone.
  5. When Mailchimp asks, choose Update existing contacts.
  6. If the Old email column has an address, that person changed their email. Mailchimp adds the new address as a new contact, so archive the old one: search the old address, open it and choose Archive.
  7. Back in the sheet, type Y in the Done column for each row you pasted (type it once and drag it down for several rows). That is the end of the job.
Nothing clears itself. Rows with a Y stay as a record. To tidy them away, choose Host list > Clear finished rows whenever you like.

Keeping the list clean

An email bounced, or someone has left the organisationThe "undeliverable", "no longer works here" and "address not found" replies.

Do this from the sheet. You rarely need Mailchimp. Mailchimp looks after its own bounces: it marks a bad address as cleaned by itself, and the weekly refresh notices.

  1. Read the reply. If it is temporary (an out-of-office message, a full mailbox), do nothing.
  2. If they have left and the reply names a replacement, add the replacement as a new row on the Contacts tab (email, name, organisation, Contact type Potential host, Local status Active).
  3. If they have just moved and you have their new address, change the email on the Contacts tab instead of removing them. It will then be passed on to Mailchimp (see the job above).
  4. If the address is dead, choose Host list > Remove a contact, type the email, and choose Yes (bounced or left). The sheet sets them to Removed and adds them to the Suppression tab, so they are never emailed or added back.
If it is a bounce from a Mailchimp send, you do not need to do anything. Only add a replacement contact if the reply names one.
Take someone off the local list but keep them on the regional list
  1. Choose Host list > Remove a contact, type their email, and choose No (they asked to come off).
  2. That sets their Local status to Asked off and adds them to Suppression.
  3. If they are in the Kaipātiki Mailchimp, unsubscribe or archive them there.
Do not touch the regional Mailchimp. They stay on it. If they also want off the regional list, they unsubscribe from it themselves.
Add the hub opt-ins to the Kaipātiki list (Dan)People who ticked Kaipātiki on the regional sign-up. Only Dan can sign in to the Kaipātiki Mailchimp.
  1. The Monday run emails Dan the new people, with a table of their email and name. The same people are on the Hub opt-ins to add tab in the sheet.
  2. Copy the rows from that tab (or the table in the email) into the Kaipātiki Mailchimp import. Choose add new only, so newer details already there are not overwritten.
  3. Straight afterwards, in the sheet choose Host list > Mark hub opt-ins as added. It moves everyone on the tab into Contacts as Active (hub opt-in) and empties the tab.
Do steps 2 and 3 in one sitting. The tab is only refilled when a run happens (Monday, or when someone chooses Run checks), so nobody new slips in between unless a run happens. Do not empty the tab by hand, or the same people come back next time.
Add a new contact by hand
  1. On the Contacts tab, go to the next empty row.
  2. Fill in Email, First name, Last name, Organisation and Phone.
  3. Set Contact type to Potential host (or Network amplifier), and Local status to Active.
  4. Check the email is not already on the sheet: search for it first (Ctrl+F).

Checks

Check that new registrations are being taggedA quick spot check before a reminder email goes out.

The website adds the Mailchimp tag when someone registers. This check makes sure that link is still working. If it breaks, people who have registered keep getting reminders, and week emails miss people.

  1. In the WordPress admin, open Events and find the one or two most recent registrations. Note each host's email address.
  2. In Mailchimp open Audience, then All contacts, and search for the address.
  3. Open the contact and look under Tags for Host 2027 (and Event Form). The website does not add a week tag yet.
  4. If a tag is missing, add it by hand and tell Dan.
Sort out the contacts marked To checkPeople nobody has decided about yet. They are not emailed until someone does.
  1. In the sheet open the Contacts tab.
  2. Click the filter button on the Local status heading and show only To check.
  3. Read the notes columns for each person.
  4. Pick from the drop-down: Active (we should email them), Asked off (they asked not to be emailed) or Removed (gone, or the address is dead). If you do not know, leave it as To check.
  5. Clear the filter when you are done.
Every Monday: the weekly checkWhen the summary email arrives.
  1. Read the summary email. It gives counts, not names.
  2. If corrections are waiting, follow Pass my changes on to Mailchimp.
  3. If there are To check contacts, follow Sort out the contacts marked To check.
  4. Hub opt-ins are Dan's job. He gets his own email.
  5. If the email reports a problem, tell Dan.
